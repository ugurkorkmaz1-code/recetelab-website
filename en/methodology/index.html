RECETELAB METHOD

The ReçeteLab Methodology

We evaluate a formulation not only in the laboratory, but together with raw materials, process conditions, cost, field performance and scalable production.

Trust in technical consulting is built through a disciplined process

01

Analyse

Product targets, current formulation, local raw materials, process conditions and field expectations are assessed together.

02

Evaluate Risk

Critical performance points, process variation, cost pressure and scale-up risks are made visible.

03

Optimise

Dosage, raw-material alternatives, additive balance and process parameters are aligned with the performance target.

04

Validate

Technical decisions are verified with laboratory tests, comparative samples and measurable acceptance criteria.

05

Transfer to Production

Pilot trials, line adaptation, mixing sequence and process parameters are scaled to real production.

06

Improve Continuously

Production and field feedback are monitored, followed by controlled revisions for cost, stability and performance.

Our quality approach

Repeatability

The target is not a one-off successful sample, but a producible system that performs consistently from batch to batch.

Measurable performance

Decisions rely on acceptance criteria, test results and process data rather than impressions.

Commercial reality

Technical performance is evaluated together with local supply, total cost, plant capacity and target market.

Confidentiality and data ownership

Formulations, costs, raw-material lists and production data remain the client’s property; NDA-based work is available.

Confidentiality and NDA approach

Formulations, production data, cost information and commercial plans are used only within the defined project scope. Mutual NDAs can be signed before technical data is shared.
