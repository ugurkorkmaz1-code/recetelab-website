Drymix Factory Setup & Commissioning Consulting

Planning a drymix mortar or tile adhesive manufacturing plant? ReceteLab supports investors and manufacturers with product portfolio planning, production-line technical requirements, formulation transfer, pilot trials and commissioning toward stable production.

Who we support

Investors establishing a new drymix mortar plant, manufacturers expanding into tile adhesive or grout production, and existing factories preparing a new production line.

From factory concept to production requirements

Define target products, production capacity and raw-material availability; establish technical requirements for silos, dosing, mixers, conveying, packing and laboratory equipment. Equipment procurement, construction and installation remain the responsibility of the project owner and appointed suppliers unless separately agreed.

Formulation development and industrial transfer

Develop or adapt agreed product formulations to locally available raw materials and the intended production process. Coordinate laboratory checks, mixing sequence, pilot batches and relevant product-performance testing within the contracted scope.

Commissioning, quality control and training

Support calibration and trial production, review batch consistency and product performance, establish practical quality-control checkpoints and operating instructions, and train relevant production and laboratory personnel.

Project stages and deliverables

Typical stages: technical assessment → product and process requirements → laboratory formulation work → production-line trials → optimization → operating and quality documentation. Exact deliverables, on-site visits, equipment responsibilities and acceptance criteria are defined in the project proposal.
