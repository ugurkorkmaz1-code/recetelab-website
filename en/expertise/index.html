Product groups we work with

Tile Adhesive Systems

C1, C2, C2TE, C2TES1 and C2TES2 targets; adhesion, open time, slip and deformability balance.

Cementitious Grouts

CG1 and CG2 focus; optimization of color, workability, surface finish, water absorption and cracking.

Self-Levelling Compounds

Flow, segregation resistance, early strength, surface quality and pump/application behaviour.

Repair Mortars

Adhesion, shrinkage control, workability, layer thickness and mechanical-performance balance.

Cementitious Waterproofing

Water resistance, adhesion, flexibility and application stability through mineral–polymer balance.

Drymix Production Systems

Raw-material feeding, dosing, mixing, homogeneity, packaging and repeatable production.
