Cementitious Systems

For tile adhesives, grouts, repair mortars, screeds and renders, we manage hydration, water retention, rheology and strength development as one connected system.

HYDRATIONRHEOLOGYSTRENGTH DEVELOPMENT
TRENAR

Product and performance scope

  • C1/C2 tile adhesives and the balance between slip and open time
  • Grouts: colour, water absorption, cracking and surface quality
  • Repair mortars: adhesion, shrinkage and early strength
  • Self-levelling compounds: flow, air control and surface regularity

Technical evaluation

  1. Assess cement type, fineness and mineral-additive hydration behaviour
  2. Balance water/binder ratio with cellulose ether, RDP and additive package
  3. Test trade-offs between early/final strength and workability
  4. Control setting and water loss for climate and application conditions

Deliverables

  • Performance target matrix by product class
  • Technical risk assessment of raw-material alternatives
  • Laboratory and field test plan
  • Production and application instructions
