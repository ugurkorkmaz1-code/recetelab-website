Cost Engineering

We optimise formulation cost, raw-material dependency and total manufacturing cost using technical evidence while protecting product quality.

COST OPTIMISATIONLOCAL RAW MATERIALSVALUE ENGINEERING
TRENAR

Where cost is created

  • Excessive polymer, cellulose ether or specialty-additive dosage
  • Expensive filler or binder selection with weak substitution options
  • High scrap, rework, energy and packing losses
  • Overdependence on one supplier or imported raw materials

Value-engineering approach

  1. Measure the real performance contribution of each raw material
  2. Build dosage-performance curves and substitution scenarios
  3. Validate local alternatives in laboratory and pilot production
  4. Compare total cost of ownership rather than formula cost alone

Deliverables

  • Detailed cost comparison of current and proposed formulas
  • Alternative-material matrix ranked by technical risk
  • Phased implementation and validation plan
  • A sustainable saving target without compromising quality
