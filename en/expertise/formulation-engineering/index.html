Formulation Engineering

We develop new products and redesign existing formulations by balancing performance, cost, application behavior and manufacturing feasibility in one technical framework.

What formulation engineering solves

  • Formulas that fail to reach target EN/TS classes or deliver inconsistent field performance
  • Open time, slip, adhesion, strength, workability and setting-balance problems
  • Performance loss or cost increase when switching to local raw materials
  • Laboratory formulas that cannot be reproduced on the production line

How we work

  1. Define product targets, benchmark products and critical performance criteria
  2. Analyse the role of binders, fillers, polymers, cellulose ethers and additives
  3. Build a structured trial matrix and compare laboratory results
  4. Validate the selected formula under pilot and full-scale production conditions

Technical deliverables

  • An applicable formula with dosage windows and raw-material alternatives
  • Quality-control tests and acceptance criteria
  • Mixing sequence, time, water ratio and packaging recommendations
  • Cost-performance comparison and a revision roadmap
