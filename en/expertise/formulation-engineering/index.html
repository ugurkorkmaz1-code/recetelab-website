What we do in this field

  • Product performance targets, market conditions and production constraints are evaluated together.
  • Raw-material functions and interactions are assessed with measurable criteria.
  • Laboratory results are validated through pilot and real production-line conditions.

Our working approach

  1. Define technical targets and the current state
  2. Analyze raw-material, formula and process data
  3. Validate through laboratory or pilot production
  4. Establish production standards, quality criteria and implementation plan

Technical outputs

  • Technical assessment and priority report
  • Raw-material–function–dosage relationship
  • Pilot-production and quality-control parameters
  • Actionable development roadmap
