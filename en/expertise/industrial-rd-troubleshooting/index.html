Industrial R&D & Technical Troubleshooting

We solve recurring production and field problems through data, experiments and root-cause analysis, then convert the solution into a permanent operating standard.

ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSISDESIGN OF EXPERIMENTSPERMANENT SOLUTION
TRENAR

Problems addressed

  • Lumping, cracking, settlement, swelling, colour and consistency variation
  • Seasonal performance changes and raw-material lot effects
  • Customer complaints, field failures and short shelf life
  • Need for a new product or technology platform

R&D method

  1. Define the problem through measurable symptoms and conditions
  2. Separate raw-material, process, packaging and application causes
  3. Test hypotheses with controlled experimental design
  4. Convert the solution into quality plans and standard instructions

Results

  • Technical report documenting root cause and evidence
  • Corrective and preventive action plan
  • Revised formula or process parameters
  • Control and monitoring system preventing recurrence
