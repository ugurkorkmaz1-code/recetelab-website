Waterproofing & Polymer Modification

For cementitious and hybrid waterproofing systems, we improve flexibility, adhesion, impermeability and application behaviour through polymer-mineral balance.

WATER IMPERMEABILITYPOLYMER MODIFICATIONADHESION
TRENAR

Systems covered

  • One- and two-component cementitious waterproofing
  • Polymer-modified flexible coatings and crack bridging
  • Wet areas, foundations, terraces and pools
  • Compatibility of primers, mesh and successive coats

Formulation approach

  1. Select polymer type and dosage against flexibility and cost targets
  2. Test capillary absorption, adhesion and resistance to water pressure
  3. Optimise filler distribution, rheology and film formation
  4. Assess packaging, storage and application-temperature risks

Technical package delivered

  • Application-specific formula and component alternatives
  • Test methods and performance acceptance criteria
  • Surface preparation and application procedure
  • Root-cause and corrective-action plan for field failures
