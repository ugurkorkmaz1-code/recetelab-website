Production & Process Optimisation

We analyse formula, equipment and operating parameters together to improve capacity, homogeneity, energy use and quality consistency.

PROCESS EFFICIENCYQUALITY CONSISTENCYCAPACITY
TRENAR

Typical improvement areas

  • Long mixing time, low capacity and bottlenecks
  • Batch-to-batch variation in density, colour or performance
  • Dosing errors, loss, rework and high scrap
  • Inefficiency in packing, shifts and product changeovers

Analysis method

  1. Map process flow and critical control points on site
  2. Evaluate time studies, batch data and quality results together
  3. Verify variables one by one through a controlled trial plan
  4. Establish standard work, control plans and monitoring indicators

Business outcomes

  • Higher net capacity and shorter cycle time
  • Lower scrap, rework and energy consumption
  • More stable product quality and traceability
  • A standardised system less dependent on individual operators
